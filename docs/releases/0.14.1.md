A replay can be shared at a moment, and the link can put whoever opens it straight into
the game.

## Replays

- **Share** on a replay's timeline makes a link to the replay: from its start or from the
  time under the playhead, to be watched or to be played from as one of its players.
  Sharing keeps the replay on your scmjs.dev account; one the account already has is not
  sent again.
- A link with a time opens the replay at that moment, paused. A link that says to play
  opens the play page on a card that names the moment and the player, takes your StarCraft
  folder there if this browser has none, and stands you in the game, paused, with a
  **Start** button.
- After a game on the play page, **Keep on my account** in the end-of-game menu keeps the
  game's replay and gives its link.

## On scmjs.dev

- The Library page keeps a map or a replay dropped on it, or chosen with **Add a file…**,
  and every row has **Copy link**.
- The Library and Replays pages are in Korean too.
