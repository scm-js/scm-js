Preferences has a filter box, can draw the interface larger, and resets one page at a
time. The View menu remembers what was ticked, and the Heal tab shows and counts the
seams it mends. Claude Desktop can now work on your maps, installed from one file with no
terminal.

## Preferences

- A filter box above the page list finds a setting on any page. Type a word and every
  section that has it is shown under its page's name, with its own controls. On Hotkeys
  the box narrows the list to a command by its name or its keys: `Ctrl+S` finds Save.
- **View ▸ Interface** draws the menus, toolbar, palettes, panels and dialogs at 115% or
  130%. The map is not enlarged, and a press still lands on the tile under the pointer.
- **View ▸ Notices** has **Keep notices until I close them**. With two or more up, a
  **Close all** sits above them.
- **Reset this page** puts back only the page in front, and **Reset everything…** asks
  first. Neither changes the Privacy tick.
- New scenario, Saving and Recovery are on a new **Files** page. General keeps Language,
  Startup, Maps and Privacy.
- The plugins' pages are listed by the plugin's name.

## View menu

- Grid, Location Names, Animate Water, Animate Units and Remastered Graphics come back as
  they were left. The three ticks that set what they started with are gone from
  Preferences ▸ View, and a choice made there is carried over.
- Where the system asks for reduced motion, the two animations start off until you tick
  them.

## Terrain

- The Heal tab says what a seam is, draws the seams on the map while it is open, and
  counts them, for the whole map or the area marked.
- Every Heal stroke says what it did: the tiles it changed and the seams left, or why it
  changed nothing.

## Several maps

- Switching to another open map ends a unit being placed and forgets where the Blend
  brush started.

## AI apps

- Claude Desktop installs scmJS from one file: `scmjs-mcp-windows-x64.mcpb`, or
  `scmjs-mcp-macos-arm64.mcpb` / `scmjs-mcp-macos-x64.mcpb` on a Mac, on this release's
  page. It asks for your maps folder and needs no terminal or Node.js.
- Ask the AI to sign you in to scmjs.dev: it opens the sign-in page in your browser, with
  no terminal.
- `scmjs mcp --folder <folder>` names the folder the tools work in, for an app that starts
  the server somewhere else.
- The AI tools guide starts with setting up Claude Desktop, then example requests with
  what they give back, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Plugins

- Aftermath 0.27.2: the replay timeline and its charts follow the pointer at a larger
  interface size.
- scmjs.dev 1.41.1: its Preferences page is listed before the Account dialog has been
  opened.

## For plugin authors

- At 115% and 130% your dialogs and panels are drawn larger with the rest of the
  interface. A pointer's `clientX` / `clientY` and `getBoundingClientRect()` are in page
  pixels, while `clientWidth` and the lengths you set are in the element's own. Turn a
  pointer position into a place in your element with the rectangle alone,
  `(e.clientX - rect.left) / rect.width * element.clientWidth`, or divide by
  `element.currentCSSZoom`. A tooltip function's `offsetX` / `offsetY` are page pixels
  too.
- **Reset everything…** calls a Preferences page's `reset()` when the page has been shown
  since the dialog opened, even with another page in front.
- `animateWater`, `animateUnits` and `hdGraphics` are no longer fields of `Preferences`.
  Read them with `api.view.flags()`. The grid, location names, the two animations and
  Remastered graphics are now remembered between sessions, so a plugin that changes one
  for a moment should set it back.
- A notice stays up, whatever time it was given, for someone who keeps notices until
  they close them.
- The API version stays 1.
