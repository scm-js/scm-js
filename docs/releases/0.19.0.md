## Comparing maps

- **Tools ▸ Compare Maps…** compares the open map with another open map, or with itself
  as it is now, so that everything you change from then on is shown. The dialog counts the
  ground, units and locations that differ, with a button for each place that goes there.
- **View ▸ Differences** draws them on the map and follows your edits: changed ground in
  magenta, what only this map has in green, what only the other has in red, and what moved
  or changed in yellow. Each open map keeps its own comparison when you switch tabs.
- Comparing changes neither map. Triggers, strings, players and settings are not compared
  here; `scmjs map diff` lists those from a terminal.

## Replays

- Aftermath 0.28.0 writes an analysed game's build order with the supply each step was
  begun at (`9 Spawning Pool`), under a card that names the opening.
- **View ▸ Replay Analysis** has a Macro tab: when each production building stood idle
  while there was money to spend, and when workers stopped.

## Plugins

## For plugin authors

- `@scm-js/core` exports the comparison the editor draws from: `diffChanges` for what
  differs from one map to another, `changedPlaces` for the differing ground joined into
  boxes, and `unitChanges`, `locationChanges` and `anyChange`.
- The API version stays 1.
